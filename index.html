A LITTLE CONTEXT

At the intersection of operations and systems.

I solve operations problems. Sometimes the fix is better data, sometimes a better process, sometimes a tool. Usually it's a mix of the three.

I didn't start in data. I trained as a civil engineer and studied finance. My first jobs were on geotechnical sites and in a classroom, teaching engineering. Then I moved into interiors, studied design in Florence and spent four years managing projects. That's where I saw how often projects slip because information doesn't reach the right person in time.

I joined DesignCafe in 2018 as a data analyst and I'm now Associate Director, four promotions later. Along the way I built the systems we use to track 2,500+ projects, helped cut the cost of missing and damaged materials by more than half, and built two analytics teams.

I use technology, AI included, when it's the simplest way to fix the problem. Often a clearer process does more than a new tool.
